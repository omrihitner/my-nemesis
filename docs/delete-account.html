Delete Your Account — My Nemesis

My Nemesis is developed by Omri Hitner. This page explains how to permanently delete your account and the data associated with it.

How to request deletion

Option 1 — In the app (instant):

  1. Open My Nemesis and go to My Profile.
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm by tapping Delete Forever.

Your account is deleted immediately — no waiting period.

Option 2 — By email (if you no longer have the app installed):

Email omrihitner@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account, with the subject "Delete my account." Requests are processed within 7 days.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your profile (username, display name, profile photo record)
  • Your login credentials
  • Photos you submitted (database records)
  • Messages you sent in group chats
  • Scores you gave as a judge
  • Your memberships in all groups

This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

What may be retained

Aggregate, non-identifying gameplay history within a group (for example, that "a player" won a given day) may remain as part of that group's shared history, since it belongs to the group rather than to any one account. Uploaded photo image files may take longer to be fully purged from storage than the account record itself.
